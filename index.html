Questions brands usually ask.

Are you a real person, or is this AI-generated?
Real person, real camera, every time. I use AI behind the scenes for transcription and editing speed, but the face, the voice, and the delivery are mine.
What deal structures do you work with?
CPM and flat-fee paid ad deliverables to start, with room to move to hybrid or retainer once we've worked together and there's a track record to point to. Happy to talk specifics on a call.
What's your niche?
Health tech and AI: wearables, apps, and tools, demoed and explained in under 60 seconds. I use these products daily, so I talk like a user, not a script. Comfortable outside the niche too if the brief is a good fit.
Who owns the content?
Standard usage terms, agreed before I film. Exclusivity, paid usage duration, and raw footage access are all negotiable and get set upfront, not after delivery.
Do you post it yourself, or just deliver the file?
Either. I'll deliver a finished file for you to run as an ad, or post from a dedicated account if that's the structure you need.
