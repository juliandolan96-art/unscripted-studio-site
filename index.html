FAQ

Questions brands ask us.

Is the content AI-generated or real creators?
AI-generated, directed by humans — and we're upfront about that because it's the advantage. Our creators are fully consistent digital faces: same person in every video, unlimited takes, no scheduling, no reshoots. The strategy, scripting, and hook selection behind every clip is human, built on patterns proven on our own multi-million-view account.
Who owns the final content?
You do — fully, from day one. Every delivered video comes with full commercial rights: run it as an ad, repost it, recut it, use it a year from now. No usage windows, no renewal fees, no whitelisting negotiations.
How do you handle brand guidelines and product accuracy?
The brief covers your brand voice, visual guidelines, and product details, and your actual product imagery is worked into the content. Every batch goes through a quality pass for accuracy before delivery, and you review before anything is final.
What does the revision process look like?
You review each delivered batch and flag anything that misses. Because our creators are digital, revisions are fast — a new take costs hours, not another shoot. Revision rounds depend on your plan, and monthly clients get priority turnaround.
How do you handle AI disclosure requirements?
We stay current on platform policies (Meta and TikTok both have AI-content labeling options) and flag what applies to your use case. Disclosure norms are evolving, and we build content that performs with or without the label — authenticity of the hook matters more than how the footage was made.
